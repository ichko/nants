What we had to add to its senses

Three things were added to the plain Langton-style senses. The compass: the ant's displacement from the cell it woke on, rotated into its own frame and scaled by half the field, then encoded as sines and cosines at frequencies 1, 2, 4, 8, cartesian and polar, 35 numbers; a place on the picture the ant can name wherever it walks. The clock and the facing were also fed in and later shown to be decorative. The sketch: the ant wanders off its landmark and turns; the dashed line is its displacement in its own frame, and the waves are the encoding, the dot showing where the current x lands. Position is two numbers; the encoding is what makes them usable by a one-layer net: fed the raw two, the loss stalls at 0.10.
