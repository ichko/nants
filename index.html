Neural Ants(NAnts)

NAnts are to Langton's ant what NCAs (neural cellular automatons) are to CAs (cellular automatons): the hand written rule, learned instead.

    How it works

    one ant, one step

    1. 1

      It looks

      At the 3×3 patch under its feet. Nothing further, ever.

    2. 2

      It decides

      One small network. 93 numbers in, 128 hidden, 19 out.

    3. 3

      It paints and turns

      Adds to the cell below, then turns left, straight on, or right.

    4. 4

      It steps forward

      Six thousand times over. A gecko comes out.

    It knows two more things: how far it has walked from where it woke up, and how far through the run it is. That is the whole of its world.

    • No memory. Nothing carries from one step to the next. Every decision is made afresh.
    • No map. Only the distance walked from its own starting cell, the way an ant counts its steps home.
    • No talking. Ants never signal each other. They share a field, and that is all.
    • One brain, any crowd. The same 14,483 numbers run every ant. One ant draws the gecko slowly, nine draw it quickly.
    • Learned, not written. What to paint comes from backprop. Where to walk comes from trial and error, because a turn cannot be differentiated.

    Train your own

    a couple of hours on a gpu

    1. 1

      Get it

      git clone https://github.com/ichko/nants
cd nants
uv sync
    2. 2

      Grow something

      uv run python starter/my_nants.py

      Change the emoji at the top of starter/my_nants.py to grow something else. It writes a folder under out/ you can watch while it works. Stop it whenever you like — the best brain is already saved.

    3. 3

      See what it made

      uv run nants-crowd out/<datetime>

      One to nine ants a square, with a piece bitten out halfway to see it grow back. There are seven more of these.
