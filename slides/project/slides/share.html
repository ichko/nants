Can ants share a gecko?

Two colonies of ten ants, both running the gecko brain, both started facing up, on one shared field. Their starting points move closer: 26 cells is about one gecko's width, so the two just touch; at 16 and 8 the pictures overlap and the ants have to paint over each other's work; at 4 the two starts are nearly the same point. Each colony paints relative to its own start. 10 000 steps; the red squares are the ants, slow at first, then sped up and faded.
