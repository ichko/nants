Spawning colonies

six brains · random spots · random headings

Twelve colonies of four ants each, dropped at random cells of one 160 by 160 field, one colony waking every 600 steps. Each colony gets one of the six trained brains, gecko, ant, butterfly, turtle, octopus or snail, and a random heading. Each paints its creature relative to its own start and heading, so the field fills with animals in every pose; where two starts landed close the pictures overlap and the colonies keep repainting over each other. 12 000 steps, to show that the pictures hold; the red squares are the ants, slow at first, then sped up and faded.
