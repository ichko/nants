Robustness

Six colonies, six brains, one field: gecko, ant, butterfly, turtle, octopus, snail. For the first 1 500 steps they paint as usual. Then an eraser, a disc twelve cells wide, sweeps once across the whole field in five slow rows over 3 000 steps, wiping every channel under it to blank white, scratch space included. The ants keep walking; the ones under the eraser lose what they had written. When the eraser leaves, they get 2 500 more steps and repaint the animals from whatever survived. Every brain reads the field it is standing on, so a hole is just more work. The red squares are the ants, slow at first, then sped up and faded.
