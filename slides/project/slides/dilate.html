Adding diffusion and decay

NAnts Lab

interactive sandbox

NAnts website

izhv.eu/nants

Ten random colonies from the lab's engine with two field rules on top of the ants' writes: diffusion, each cell blends toward a blur of its neighbours every step, and decay, every cell fades a little toward blank. Both strengths are random per colony, as is everything else: number of ants, where they start, the net's shape, activation and init, how the field is seeded. Nothing is trained. The point of the grid is the variety of behaviour and dynamics these two rules add: fronts that spread, blobs that swell, textures that keep churning. The buttons go to the live lab at izhv.eu/nants/lab and to the project website at izhv.eu/nants.
