0 or 1
any number, in multiple channels
Two clicks. First the board as Langton had it: cells flip to 1 or back to 0, one at a time, outright. Then the same board, the same flicker, but each cell sets off from the 0 or 1 it had and keeps rising and falling on its own; the board tilts into a stack, and more sheets appear beneath, each its own channel. That stack is what our ants read and write.
