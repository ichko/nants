Other agents that paint

Neural Physarium

johnowhitaker.dev/mini-projects/physarium

A slime-mould swarm whose agents are a trained neural net. Trained end to end to paint a target image.

Fluoddity

github.com/aphid91/Fluoddity

A physarum-style particle system for real-time play. Trails decay and diffuse, particles steer by density. Not trained, tuned by hand.

Two neighbours of this project. Neural Physarium, by Jonathan Whitaker: agents in the style of a physarum, slime mould, transport model, with a small neural network deciding each agent's turn and what pigment it lays down; the whole swarm is trained by gradient descent through differentiable splatting to paint a target, so it is the closest cousin to our ants. Fluoddity, by aphid91: a 2D particle system built on Sage Jenson's physarum model, where particles leave trails that decay and diffuse and steer by sensing trail density; it is a hand-tuned toy for real-time exploration, with a WebGL demo, not a trained one. Ours differs from both in that every ant is a memoryless net reading a continuous multi-channel field in its own frame, and in the compass.
