Before training: random brains

 

The Nant Lab's eight presets side by side, recorded from its engine: colonies of ants with random, untrained brains on a 128 by 128 field, coloured by the strongest channel. Every ant reads its patch through the same kernels as the trained ants; the presets differ in how many ants, where they start, the net's shape and init, and whether the field diffuses or fades. Nothing here is learned, and it already draws; training is what turns this into a picture we chose. The live lab is at the demo link if a viewer asks.
