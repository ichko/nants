Origin

Christopher Langton

Santa Fe Institute, 1989 · Cary Herz, CC BY-SA 3.0

Coined the term artificial life.

Organized the first Artificial Life workshop, Los Alamos, 1987: Workshop on the Synthesis and Simulation of Living Systems.

As a graduate student at the University of Michigan, created Langton’s ant and Langton’s loop.

read more: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Langton

Two minutes. He is here to explain the name and the spirit: life as behaviour, not as stuff. If asked: conscientious objector, orderly at Mass General wheeling bodies to the morgue, then night-shift programming where he ran Conway's Life and felt something alive in the room; the hang-glider crash over Grandfather Mountain broke 35 bones and he watched his mind come back piece by piece; first artificial world on an Apple II; Langton's loops 1984; the lambda parameter, PhD Michigan 1990; son of the novelist Jane Langton; left the field in the late 1990s.
