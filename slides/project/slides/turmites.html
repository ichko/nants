A turmite is a Turing machine let loose on a grid: a head with a few states, a tape of coloured cells, a table saying what to write, which way to turn and what state to be in next. Langton's ant is the one-state two-colour case. This one has one state and four colours: read the colour under you, turn left on the first two, right on the other two, paint the next colour, step. LLRR grows a symmetric shape that keeps its shape as it grows. The strip on the left is its whole rule.
