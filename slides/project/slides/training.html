Training

One round of training, a stage per click. Run: the colony walks a hundred steps and draws. Compare: the drawing against the target, mean squared error. Credit: the writes are differentiable, so their gradient comes back by backprop through the field; the turns are sampled, so they are credited by REINFORCE, rounds that scored better made more likely. Update: a small step of the optimiser. Repeat from a blank board, the ants back at the centre. The sixth click runs round after round; the curve is the loss falling. The drawing here is a sketch of what training does, not the real optimiser.
