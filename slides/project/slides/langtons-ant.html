Seven clicks on one slide. The rule appears on the fifth; the sixth starts it. Each stage starts the moment its click reveals it: the board fills and empties once, the ant turns through all four headings once, the click after the rule starts it from the centre. The seventh click switches to a fine board running about 840 steps a second: chaos for ten or eleven seconds, then the highway appears and runs off the edge.
